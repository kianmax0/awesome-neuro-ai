# Community: people, events, and resource collections

> Find research discussions, conferences, and complementary reading or training resources.

**For:** Newcomers looking for accessible conversations and training routes, and researchers following current work or finding venues to discuss it.

**Start with:** Browse Brain Inspired for research conversations, World Wide for seminars, the related lists for a learning route, or CCN and COSYNE programs for active research topics. The [training programs](#training-programs) below provide another route for prepared students and researchers; participation, deadlines, and registration conditions change by edition.

| Your goal | A useful route | Background and access |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hear what researchers are asking | Choose a Brain Inspired conversation and use the [glossary](../start-here/glossary.md) for unfamiliar terms. | No coding needed; individual conversations vary in technical depth and access. |
| Find training material or a broader reading list | Browse the related collections and [training programs](#training-programs), then choose a [learning pathway](../start-here/pathways.md). | Follow the source's prerequisites, application requirements, and access notes. |
| Follow active research | Explore conference programs and connect a talk or paper to a [topic](../topics/README.md). | Programs are useful reading maps; participation is edition-specific. |
| Teach or organize a reading group | Pair a [learning resource](../learn/README.md) with a [reading or modeling exercise](../projects/README.md). | State the audience, preparation, discussion question, and expected output. |

These are routes to material and conversations, rather than an exhaustive directory of people or institutions. Check a source's own terms before reusing teaching material or recordings.

## Related lists

- [Awesome NeuroAI Papers](https://github.com/CYHSM/awesome-neuro-ai-papers) - A broader reading list of papers and reviews at the intersection of deep learning and neuroscience.
- [NeuroAI Trainee Resources](https://github.com/8erberg/NeuroAI_Trainee_Resources) - Training resources and community entry points for students exploring NeuroAI.
- [Awesome Computational Neuroscience](https://github.com/kianmax0/awesome-computational-neuroscience) - Companion collection covering mathematical models, neural computation, simulation tools, and neural-data analysis.

## Conferences and research conversations

- [Cognitive Computational Neuroscience (CCN)](https://ccneuro.org/) - Conference connecting cognition, neuroscience, and computational models, with papers, tutorials, and community events. Newcomers can explore archived programs; researchers can follow each edition's calls and participation requirements.
- [Computational and Systems Neuroscience (COSYNE)](https://www.cosyne.org/) - Meeting for empirical and theoretical studies of neural computation and behavior, with tutorials and focused workshops. Useful for placing NeuroAI models in a systems-neuroscience context; consult the current edition for registration and submission conditions.
- [Brain Inspired](https://braininspired.co/) - Research-interview podcast exploring neuroscience and AI, with episode pages and discussions of models and methods. Public listening material is available, while full archive access and the Discord community are advertised as supporter benefits.
- [World Wide (formerly World Wide Neuro)](https://world-wide.org/neuro) - Free discovery platform for neuroscience seminars, research events, and recorded talks, including computational neuroscience. Check each original organizer's page for participation conditions; saving items requires a free account.

## Training programs

These programs complement the open materials in [Learn](../learn/README.md). Check the current edition's audience, prerequisites, application process, attendance requirements, and funding before planning participation.

- [Brains, Minds and Machines](https://www.mbl.edu/education/advanced-research-training-courses/course-offerings/brains-minds-and-machines) - Intensive three-week course at the Marine Biological Laboratory connecting neuroscience, cognitive science, and AI through lectures, computational labs, and research projects. Aimed at graduate students, postdocs, and faculty with a strong background in a relevant discipline; application and full-course attendance are required, and support terms should be checked for the chosen edition.
