# Awesome NeuroAI [![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge.svg)](https://awesome.re)

> Curated resources for exploring natural and artificial intelligence.

Discover how neuroscience can inform AI, how AI models can help explain brains and behavior, and what principles intelligent systems might share. Find an accessible introduction, bridge a gap in your background, explore a scientific question, or choose material for a project.

## What brings you here?

| Your goal | Start with | A useful first outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Understand what NeuroAI is | [A no-code introduction](start-here/README.md#what-is-neuroai) | Explain one example of a connection between brains and AI. |
| Bring an AI or ML background to neuroscience | [The ML-to-neuroscience route](start-here/pathways.md#machine-learning-to-neuroscience) | Understand what a neural measurement means and what a model can explain. |
| Bring a neuroscience background to AI | [The neuroscience-to-ML route](start-here/pathways.md#neuroscience-to-machine-learning) | Understand training objectives, model fitting, and held-out evaluation. |
| Learn through a concrete exercise | [Projects](projects/README.md) | Produce a reading note, comparison figure, or documented modeling exercise. |
| Find a research direction or evaluate a claim | [Topics](topics/README.md) | Connect a question to papers, measurements, alternatives, and evaluations. |
| Find software, data, or an evaluation | [Resources](resources/README.md) | Choose materials that match a task and its constraints. |
| Teach, run a reading group, or follow discussions | [Learning routes](start-here/pathways.md) and [Community](community/README.md) | Assemble a session or find a conversation to follow. |

Choose the route that fits your current question. You can read an overview without coding, go directly to a dataset, or revisit foundations while working on a project.

## Explore by scientific question

| Question | Topic |
| --- | --- |
| How can we compare artificial models with brains and behavior? | [Comparing brains and models](topics/README.md#comparing-brains-and-models) |
| How do systems represent sensory information and make predictions? | [Perception and representations](topics/README.md#perception-and-representations) |
| How can experience and feedback change a network? | [Learning rules and predictive coding](topics/README.md#learning-rules-and-predictive-coding) |
| How do memory, planning, and decision-making work together? | [Memory, planning, and decision-making](topics/README.md#memory-planning-and-decision-making) |
| How can systems transfer knowledge and retain what they learn? | [Generalization and adaptation](topics/README.md#generalization-and-adaptation) |

The [topic guide](topics/README.md) explains the selective coverage and links each question to relevant resources.

## Browse by resource type

- **[Learn](learn/README.md):** Public lectures, programming and mathematics, neuroscience and dynamics texts, reinforcement learning, cognitive modeling, reviews, courses, and tutorials.
- **[Tools](resources/tools.md):** Task environments, neural-population and spiking models, representation analysis, continual learning, embodied control, and neural-data infrastructure.
- **[Datasets](resources/datasets.md):** Neural and behavioral measurements, converted event-based inputs, and archives for finding further collections.
- **[Benchmarks](resources/benchmarks.md):** Brain–model comparison, population modeling, response prediction, decoding, continual learning, and neuromorphic evaluation.
- **[Community](community/README.md):** Related lists, conferences, research conversations, seminar discovery, and training programs.

Resource descriptions live in one place. Topics, learning routes, and projects link to those entries so their access notes and limitations can be maintained together.
