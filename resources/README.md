> Find a tool, a measurement, or an evaluation for the question you want to study.

**For:** Readers choosing software, neural or behavioral data, or an evaluation for a first exercise or a research project.

**Start with:** State what you want to measure or compare, then check the resource page for its purpose, prerequisites, and any stated access or compatibility caveats. Distinguish recorded neural or behavioral measurements from model-generated spike inputs; a dataset portal helps locate collections and is not itself one uniform dataset.
