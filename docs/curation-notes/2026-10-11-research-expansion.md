## Verification and limits

- Nine additions; seventeen paper entries total. Existing eight entries retain their titles, URLs, authors/year/journal descriptions, and caveats.
- Existing anchors `comparing-brains-and-models`, `learning-rules-and-predictive-coding`, and `memory-planning-and-decision-making` remain unchanged. New topic table targets correspond to headings in the page.
- Newly added links were selected from publisher, proceedings, author, or institutional full-text sources. Access observations above describe this check, not a guarantee of future availability.
- No added paper is presented as an unpublished preprint result: the two repository/manuscript links explicitly identify their versions and retain their published article metadata. The conceptual credit-assignment entry is explicitly a perspective.
- No numerical benchmark comparison, general speed claim, or claim that a model uniquely identifies a brain mechanism was added. This is a bounded reading expansion, not an exhaustive or ranked literature review.
