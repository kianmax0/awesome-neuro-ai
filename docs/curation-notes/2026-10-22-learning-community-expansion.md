# Learning and community expansion: 2026-10-22

## Selection

Five additions to [Learn](../../learn/README.md) address specific gaps: reinforcement learning as a bridge to psychology and neuroscience, probabilistic cognitive modeling, mathematical background for neural dynamics, a review of trained recurrent networks, and a public lecture for readers who do not code. Two additions to [Community](../../community/README.md) provide seminar discovery and an application-based interdisciplinary training route. These choices complement the existing introductions and courses rather than adding another general machine-learning curriculum. Background guidance and suggested reading outcomes are editorial judgments, not measured teaching-effectiveness claims.

The original 15 Learn entries and 6 Community entries, including their descriptions and URLs, are retained verbatim. New sections retain the original headings and provide anchors for internal navigation. Each new resource has one canonical entry; other pages should link to its section rather than repeat its description.

## Verification boundary

This is documentation and source review. No software was installed, no notebooks or browser exercises were executed, and no datasets were downloaded. No accounts, registrations, applications, or external records were created or changed. Textbook availability does not grant permission to redistribute its contents; source terms continue to apply.

- Whitespace checks passed. Validation covers these pages and additions; repository-wide navigation integration and checks belong to the combined expansion review.
