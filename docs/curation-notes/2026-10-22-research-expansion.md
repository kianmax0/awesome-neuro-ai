# Research paper expansion — 2026-10-22

Scope: `topics/README.md`. Added nine published papers to the seventeen existing primary entries. The additions broaden auditory and multisensory perception, recurrent dynamics, dopamine/value coding, human–network continual learning, and synaptic-plasticity modeling. The seven existing topic headings and anchors are retained. Background reading and project routes now reflect these additions, including a selected-paper collection under neural dynamics.

This is a bounded reading expansion, not an exhaustive literature review or a reproduction report. Inclusion favors distinct scientific questions and evidence types. Metadata, abstracts, and selected primary-text sections were checked at the depths recorded below; no implementation, supplementary-code audit, or independent reanalysis was performed.
