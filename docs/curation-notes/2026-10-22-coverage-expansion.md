## Important distinctions retained

- The Spiking Heidelberg Datasets contain model-generated cochlear events, not recordings of biological neurons; SHD's full test partition is not entirely speaker-disjoint.
- Narratives' neural-data license does not make its associated story audio freely licensed.
- Brainwide coverage in the IBL release is assembled across sessions and animals.
- The lfads-torch license restricts use and modification; its entry does not call it freely modifiable open-source software.
- Algonauts 2025 is a completed competition with a stated post-challenge route; this review does not verify authenticated submission operability.
- NeuroBench algorithm metrics and physical hardware energy or latency are different evaluations.
- A CSHL research program was not relabeled as a standalone course; a separately verified public webinar supplies the introductory route.
