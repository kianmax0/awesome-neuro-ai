# Practical resource expansion: 2026-10-22

## Scope and selection

Added four tools, three datasets, and three evaluations to fill practical gaps in the seven scientific themes: population dynamics, musculoskeletal control, brain–model comparisons, decision-task recordings, naturalistic language, temporal spiking classification, and adaptation. Avalanche is included for concrete comparisons of replay and elastic weight consolidation (EWC), rather than as a general machine-learning library. CORe50 has one canonical home under benchmarks; its data and protocols are not counted as separate resources. Existing Tonic, Brain-Score, Meta NeuroAI, and neural-data collections remain distinct entries.

The resource index links to every new entry, and the three type pages provide internal comparison-table links and H3 resource anchors. The original 17 practical-resource descriptions and all existing headings are retained. The seven scientific-question links in the resource index remain unchanged.

## Verification boundary

This review opened official documentation, maintainer repositories, dataset-owner pages, release metadata, and associated papers. Software examples, stated dependencies, licenses, access conditions, and evaluation status were inspected. No software was installed, notebooks or training runs executed, dataset archives downloaded, benchmark predictions submitted, or authenticated evaluation workflow tested. Inclusion and proposed routes are editorial judgments; documentation review does not establish runtime compatibility, reproduced performance, or a biological mechanism.

Sources were reviewed on 2026-10-22. Main-branch files and latest documentation can change; the lfads-torch license and dependency evidence below is also linked to the reviewed commit because its restrictions materially affect reuse.

## Datasets: primary evidence

| Resource | Scope and reuse verified | Access, licensing, and limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [IBL Brainwide Map](../../resources/datasets.md#ibl-brainwide-map) | The [release documentation](https://docs.internationalbrainlab.org/notebooks_external/2025_data_release_brainwidemap.html) and [2025 paper](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-09235-0) describe spike-sorted Neuropixels activity during a standardized mouse visual decision task, with stimulus, choice, feedback, wheel, and pose variables for encoding/decoding. | The [IBL-managed AWS registry](https://registry.opendata.aws/ibl-brain-wide-map/) declares CC BY 4.0 and public S3 access without an AWS account; [ONE instructions](https://docs.internationalbrainlab.org/notebooks_external/data_download.html) explain public access. Sorted units include multiunit clusters; apply quality criteria, check modality availability, and use current release metadata because synchronization and video corrections are documented. |
| [Narratives](../../resources/datasets.md#narratives) | The [owner page](https://snastase.github.io/datasets/ds002345) and [2021 data descriptor](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41597-021-01033-3) describe naturalistic spoken-story fMRI, BIDS data, annotations, and feature-to-BOLD comparisons. Participant/story coverage varies; the collection is not one complete shared-stimulus experiment. | OpenNeuro ds002345 [metadata](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/OpenNeuroDatasets/ds002345/master/dataset_description.json) declares CC0. The [stimuli README](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/OpenNeuroDatasets/ds002345/master/stimuli/README) separately states that story media are copyrighted and supplied for non-profit, noncommercial scholarly use under fair-use/fair-dealing guidance. The CC0 declaration is not generalized to unrestricted audio reuse. Raw MRI, derivatives, and media have different retrieval paths; none were downloaded. |
| [Spiking Heidelberg Datasets](../../resources/datasets.md#spiking-heidelberg-datasets) | The [owner page](https://zenkelab.org/resources/spiking-heidelberg-datasets-shd/) and [paper's author version](https://arxiv.org/html/1910.07407) identify spoken-digit SHD and speech-command SSC inputs converted by an artificial cochlea into 700 spike channels. These are modeled inputs rather than biological neural recordings. | The owner and [release README](https://zenkelab.org/datasets/shd/README.md) declare CC BY 4.0 and describe public HDF5 files. SHD supplies train/test, while SSC also supplies validation. SHD test combines two held-out speakers and samples from speakers present in training. The current owner page and v1.0 README give different SHD partition counts, so exact sample counts are omitted; archive contents were not inspected to resolve the difference. |

## Access observations

The Algonauts Google Form could be read through a direct HTTP request after the browser text tool rejected its redirect; no form was filled or sent. Its two linked Codabench post-challenge pages timed out during browser reads. The NeuroBench published-article page redirected to a publisher identity endpoint and the PMC copy presented a browser challenge; its author version and official documentation were used for scope verification. These access outcomes do not establish that a resource has disappeared, nor do an accessible landing page or license file establish a working installation or submission service.
